## 2026-11-11 — Weekly issues and reading allowances

- Explorer accounts include 2 research reports and 1 weekly issue. Remaining allowance is shown in **My projects**.
- The latest five weekly issues now include 25 additional X posts from their respective weeks, bringing the collection to 394 cases across 32 issues at the time of this update.
- Browse each platform's cases horizontally.

**中文：** Explorer 账户包含 2 篇研究报告和 1 期周刊，剩余额度可在 My projects 查看。最近五期周刊补充了对应周的 25 条 X 内容，本次更新时共收录 32 期、394 个案例；各平台案例支持横向浏览。

## 2026-11-10 — Research library and secure access

- Published a library of 793 bilingual reports with source tables, charts, and references; business metric snapshots remain separate.
- Weekly Explorations covers April 1 onward, with 369 cases across 32 bilingual issues at the time of this update, browsable by month.
- Migrated 6,298 verified research images to R2, with expandable tall images and report galleries.
- One-prompt Agent setup includes browser sign-in and consent, with account-bound, expiring, revocable credentials.
- Guest and Explorer reading allowances count distinct titles. Reopening the same title in either language uses no additional credit.

**中文：** 上线包含来源表格、图表和参考资料的 793 篇双语报告；周刊支持按月浏览。改进研究图片与长图阅读，完善 Agent 登录授权流程，并按独立标题计算阅读额度，同一标题切换语言或重复打开不重复扣额。
